# ⚡ Watt Watch – Urban Energy Consumption Dashboard

### Smart Energy • Sustainable Tomorrow 🌱

Watt Watch is an interactive **Power BI dashboard** designed to analyze urban energy consumption, outage patterns, estimated costs, and consumer-wise energy usage.

The dashboard helps understand **energy consumption trends across different zones and consumer types** and provides useful insights for smarter energy management.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of this project is to analyze urban energy data and provide meaningful insights into:

- ⚡ Total energy consumption
- 📈 Average peak energy usage
- 🚨 Outage minutes by zone
- 💰 Estimated energy cost
- 🏙️ Zone-wise cost comparison
- 👥 Consumer-type energy consumption
- 📅 Monthly energy consumption trends
- 🔌 Top energy-consuming meters

---

## 🖥️ Dashboard Features

### 🔹 KPI Cards

The dashboard provides key performance indicators such as:

- **Total Consumption:** 6.21M KWh
- **Average Peak Usage:** 9.42 KWh
- **Total Outage Minutes:** 1M Minutes
- **Total Estimated Cost:** 49.34M

### 🔹 Interactive Filters

Users can dynamically filter the dashboard using:

- Zone
- Consumer Type
- Date

### 🔹 Visualizations

The dashboard includes:

- **Top 5 Energy-Consuming Meters**
- **Total Cost by Zone**
- **Energy Usage by Consumer Type**
- **Outage Minutes by Zone**
- **Monthly Energy Consumption Trend**

---

## 📌 Key Insights

- **Central Zone** has the highest estimated energy cost.
- **Industrial consumers** contribute the largest share of energy consumption.
- The dashboard identifies the **top 5 energy-consuming meters** for better monitoring.
- Energy consumption varies across zones and throughout the year.
- Outage minutes can be compared across different zones to identify areas requiring attention.
